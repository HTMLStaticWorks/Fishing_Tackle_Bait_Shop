Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our tackle inventory, fresh bait, order pickup, and guided charters.

Do you stock fresh and live bait daily at the store?

Yes! We receive fresh shipments of live bait daily, including nightcrawlers, live minnows, seasonal shrimp, and cut bait. You can call ahead or stop by early in the morning before your trip to ensure your preferred bait is ready.

Can your staff help beginners choose the right tackle setup?

Absolutely. Whether you're targeting freshwater bass, trout, or coastal saltwater species, our experienced staff will pair you with the ideal rod, reel, line weight, and lure setup tailored to your experience level and budget.

How do I reserve or book a guided fishing excursion?

You can select "Guided Trip" in the enquiry form above, call our shop directly, or browse available packages on our Trips page. We will coordinate your dates, party size, and target fish, providing certified guides and all premium gear.

Do you offer in-store pickup for tackle orders?

Yes, same-day in-store pickup is available for all in-stock rods, reels, terminal tackle, and lures. Just call ahead or send us an enquiry, and our team will have your order packed and waiting at the register.

What is your return and equipment warranty policy?

All unused tackle in its original packaging can be returned or exchanged within 14 days of purchase with a receipt. For manufacturer warranty claims on reels and high-end rods, our staff will assist you with official repair or replacement.
